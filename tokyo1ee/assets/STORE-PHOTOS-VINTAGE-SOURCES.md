# Store photo sources — Harajuku / Shibuya

Checked 2026-11-04. Each store has 2–3 independently sourced venue views. Images are actual store, display or editorial photographs, not generated images. Historical years are kept in captions where applicable. Current stock and decor may differ. No claim of open licensing is made; source and photographer/provider attribution are retained.

All downloaded images were visually inspected as contact sheets. Logos, generic chain images, unrelated branches, opening-announcement flyers and duplicate crops were rejected.

## Specific verification notes

- RAGTAG Harajuku photos show the current 5-17-9 branch (2024 introduction), not the old 6-14-2 store.
- RAGTAG Shibuya exterior is mapped to the SHIBUYA heading in the official global store list. Its English street number is truncated in that source; this task does not alter the previously verified Japanese address.
- KLD images come directly from the official May 2026 opening article, obtained through its public image links. The Jente collage and opening flyers were rejected in favor of three actual official room views.
- Chicago Holiday interior is linked on the Tokyo Sanpo page specifically to the Jingumae shop, Holiday spot318094. No Harajuku/Takeshita branch images used.
- BerBerJin Collective FASHIONSNAP image credits distinguish provider BerBerJin from FASHIONSNAP staff photo. The article footer mistakenly repeats main-store address; use the existing official store-list address for routing.
- 2nd STREET exterior/entrance photos are2025 user photos on the exact Jinnan location. The owner-provided Yahoo image is a logo and was excluded.
- SULLEN uses two CHOKiCHOKi interior/editorial photographs published May 25, 2025, plus the exact location’s 2025 entrance sign. The 2013 fallback and closeup logo were excluded.
