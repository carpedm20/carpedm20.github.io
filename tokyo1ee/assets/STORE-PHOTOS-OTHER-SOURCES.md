# Store option photo sources: Akihabara and east Tokyo

Checked 2026-11-04. All local copies are max1000px WebP. Metadata preserves creator/source/license where provided. No synthetic store photos. Photos show spaces at the date of the source; fitout and stock may change.

## Selection notes

- Surugaya photos show the current 3-15-4 2F location after its September 2026 move.
- Yellow Submarine is the dedicated RPG shop at 4-6-1 6F; the separate Radio Kaikan tenant photo is labeled on Radio Kaikan only.
- Geestore photos are after its 2020 move to Onoden 4F; older official collages were rejected.
- SHOSEN photos show its October 2026 pre-opening.
- Love Merci images are nonexplicit facade and packaged clothing rack from a 2022 visit.
- SKAC twelvebooks and VDS galleries show their distinct book/record spaces.
- Kastori photos are from the current location after its 2023 move.
- Nobori has a second exterior/window view rather than mislabeling another shop as its interior.
