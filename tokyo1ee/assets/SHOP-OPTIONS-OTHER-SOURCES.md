# Shop options: Akihabara and east Tokyo

Verified 2026-11-04. These are choices for a browsing block, not appointments or a claim that every shop can be visited. No 2026-12-30–2027-01-03 special opening is guaranteed. Public shop sources are linked in the data. Clothing and costume stock is not verified. No images were downloaded for this change.

## Akihabara culture (6 choices)

- **Mandarake Complex** — [official store](https://www.mandarake.co.jp/dir/cmp/), 3-11-12 Sotokanda. Existing `mandarake` guide/photo correspond to this building. Focus label emphasizes used books and magazines rather than figurines.
- **Mandarake Complex2** — [official store](https://www.mandarake.co.jp/dir/cmp2/index-en.html), 3-12-2 Sotokanda. Separate building: cards 1–2F, games 3F, CD/DVD/Blu-ray 4–5F. [Official opening announcement](https://edith.mandarake.co.jp/information/newopen/cmp2/) dates opening to 2025-08-30. Not a second floor of Complex.
- **Suruga-ya Akihabara Game Store** — [official branch list](https://www.suruga-ya.com/en/shoplist), 3-15-4 Sotokanda, 2F. Retro/current games, strategy books and game magazines. Exact branch specified; not the figure shop.
- **RECOfan Akihabara SEEKBASE** — [official branch page](https://www.recofan.co.jp/map/SEEKBASE.html), 13-1 Kanda Neribeicho, SEEKBASE. LP/CD and music books. Separate from Shibuya branch.
- **Yellow Submarine Akihabara RPG Shop** — [official company/store directory](https://yellowsubmarine.co.jp/company/), 4-6-1 Sotokanda, Shiota Tour Akihabara 6F. Boardgames/TRPG. [Official relocation announcement](https://yellowsubmarine.co.jp/news/news0926/) documents 2023-10-28 relocation. Do not reuse obsolete 1-11-5 address or confuse it with the Radio Kaikan Main Mint store.
- **SHOSEN BASE LAB** — [JR East Urban Development operator announcement](https://www.jrtk.jp/seekbase/info/detail_00179/), SEEKBASE building 01, unit 1-2, 13-1 Kanda Neribeicho. Preopened 2026-10-10; books and miscellaneous goods about games, food/drink, history and hobbies. December 2026 full opening is only planned, so data says preopened rather than claiming the full opening happened.

Not included: 2nd BASE camera shop. Its [official store page](https://www.2ndbase.jp/html/page17.html) explicitly lists Wednesday and year-end/New Year closures. The itinerary's Akihabara day is Wednesday 12/30, so it is a poor default recommendation even though the store is otherwise relevant.

## Akihabara couple retail and general alternatives (5 choices)

Only the first two entries are adult shops. The remaining three are clearly labeled general retail alternatives, not falsely presented as five dedicated adult shops. No explicit imagery or product descriptions are added.

- **M's Akihabara** — [official branch/floor page](https://www.ms-online.co.jp/page/akihabara-store), 1-15-13 Sotokanda, Taiheido B1–6F. Official page includes couple goods, lingerie and costumes; existing `ms` guide belongs to this location.
- **Love Merci Akihabara** — [official shop X](https://x.com/akibalovemerci). Address 1-2-7 Sotokanda also corroborated by [Yahoo Maps branch entry](https://map.yahoo.co.jp/v3/place/ysmHp85mhdM?from_srv=loco_web). An independent adult retailer at the same street address as Onoden; not the same shop as Gee!store.
- **Gee!store Akiba / COSPATIO** — [official shop page](https://www.geestore.com/geenet/geestore/akiba), 1-2-7 Sotokanda, Onoden building 4F. COSPA, COSPATIO, TRANTRIP apparel/costume brands. General cosplay store, not an adult shop. Current 2026 announcements appear on the official branch page.
- **COSPA Official Store at Atre Akihabara** — [official facility tenant page](https://www.atre.co.jp/akihabara/shop/9311/), Atre Akihabara 1, 3F. Official character apparel/accessories; distinct from the Onoden Gee!store location.
- **Don Quijote Akihabara** — [official branch](https://www.donki.com/store/shop_detail.php?add=1&shop_id=98), 4-3-3 Sotokanda. General fashion/accessory/daily-goods alternative; no promise of a particular costume's availability. Official ordinary 24-hour schedule is not used to claim verified holiday operations.

## East Tokyo (6 choices, deliberately split by geography)

The first two shops share **one** SKAC building; they are not two separate neighborhood stops. Eishindo is another Kameari shop. Yoshiwara and Kuramae require a separate transit extension: they are not described as walking distance from SKAC. Data labels the areas on every entry and the cluster note explains this distinction.

- **twelvebooks** — [official about/visit page](https://twelve-books.com/pages/about), 3-26-4 Nishi-Kameari. Existing `skac` guide and `discover-skac` photo refer to this location. Art books and photography publications.
- **VDS / Vinyl Delivery Service** — [official store](https://vinyldeliveryservice.com/), same SKAC address. Official website timed out in web fetch, so physical tenancy was verified through an [Audio-Technica interview with VDS founder Rintaro Sekizuka](https://www.audio-technica.co.jp/always-listening/articles/skac/) and [Time Out's VDS store listing](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/shopping/vds). The interview identifies VDS, twelvebooks and tawks as SKAC tenants; no third retail shop is fabricated from the coffee shop.
- **Eishindo / 栄眞堂書店** — [Katsushika shopping street association member listing](https://www.katsushika-kushouren.jp/store/s35025/), 5-32-11 Kameari, north of Kameari station. New/used books, records, CDs and nostalgic miscellaneous goods.
- **Kastori Bookstore** — [official current store blog](https://kastoribookstore.blogspot.com/), **3-21-14 Senzoku**, Taito. Do not reuse its pre-2023 old 4-39-3 address. [Hypemaps bookstore guide](https://hypemaps.com/cities/tokyo/best-bookstores-comics-manga-print-shop-tokyo) explicitly lists Kastori, supporting the clickable Hypemaps recommendation badge. History/research/independent publications about sexual culture; no explicit imagery added.
- **Kakimori** — [official stores page](https://kakimori.com/pages/stores), 1-6-2 Misuji, Taito, 1F. Stationery, notebooks and ink. The same-building Inkstand service is not counted as another separate shop.
- **Douguya nobori / 道具屋nobori** — [official operator about/store page](https://fromafar.stores.jp/about), 3-7-1 Kotobuki, Taito, Kotobukicho Flower Home 1F. Tableware, glass and household objects. Suitable as a Kuramae-area pairing with Kakimori, not as a Kameari neighbor.

Do not add a Hypemaps badge to Akihabara Mandarake based on the same bookstore article: its Mandarake recommendation specifically describes the **Shibuya** branch.
