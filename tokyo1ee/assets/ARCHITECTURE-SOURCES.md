## Access decisions

- Central candidates are conditional. No confirmed itinerary, meal choices or airport timing is changed.
- 21_21's official recurring closure is December 27–January 3, covering the entire trip. Exterior-only consideration is conditional on accessible public paths; no gallery or sunken-court access is promised.
- Sayama's current Japanese operator page asks architecture visitors to refrain during New Year. Its English page lists specific blackout dates under **2025**; these were not presented as 2026–27 dates. The burial shelter is a distinct work from the chapel and community hall, with structural system **S** on the architect's page.
- Tama lists New Year closure and permits even exterior/ground-floor visits only when the library is open. The exact 2026–27 calendar could not be verified, so it is deferred, with a calendar/visit link. Ordinary weekdays or semester dates are not proof of holiday access.
- Hikari no Sono and Rurikoin are memorial facilities. Sales/columbarium tours do not establish permission for architectural tourism. No inquiry or reservation was sent.
- Cathedral 09:00–17:00 comes from a diocese notice originally published in 2020, so it is described as ordinary guidance with holiday confirmation required. The church's official wedding rules state that interior photography is normally prohibited.
- Rurikoin's official text was readable through web search, but its full page/image requests encountered Cloudflare. Its 2026-10-03 notice titled “参拝所休止” is image-only; the affected room/dates could not be read, so no whole-building closure claim was inferred. Link the current operator notices before visiting.
