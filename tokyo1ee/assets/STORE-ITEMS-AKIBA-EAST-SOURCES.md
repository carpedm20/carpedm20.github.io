## Evidence and limitations

- Surugaya: The [official Akihabara Game branch](https://www.suruga-ya.jp/feature/realstore/akihabara_game/index.html) confirms retro games, current games, game magazines and guides. The two box images come from official chain product listings, not claimed branch stock. Box/manual inclusion and game condition vary.
- RECOfan: [Official SEEKBASE branch page](https://www.recofan.co.jp/map/SEEKBASE.html) confirms CDs/LPs/new and used music. The two album sleeves are artist-provided examples of records to look for. Neither exact artist/title nor edition availability is verified at RECOfan. The data explicitly says so.
- Yellow Submarine: Two Oink titles shown in its [official brand category](https://shop.yellowsubmarine.co.jp/products/list?category_id=702040815). Branch stock and player count must be checked; these are not represented as specifically two-player games.
- SHOSEN BASE LAB: [Official landlord launch notice](https://www.jrtk.jp/seekbase/info/detail_00179/) confirms game, history, food/drink and hobby-book categories. SEGA hardware history is a book previously promoted/sold by the Shosen chain; Sherlock cookbook is in its [Shosen + 10 books reprint project](https://shosen.tokyo/). BASE LAB title-level inventory remains unknown. Neither earlier event nor another branch is presented as a BASE LAB event.
- Love Merci: The store official account is https://x.com/akibalovemerci. Exact merchandise could not be verified from accessible primary material. The condom packaging and care-wash bottle are manufacturer examples, expressly labelled as unconfirmed for both store handling and stock. No graphic sexual imagery.
- Geestore: [Official Akiba branch](https://www.geestore.com/geenet/geestore/akiba) confirms COSPATIO costumes/support accessories; rabbit and cat headbands are actual COSPATIO products. Cat ears have an online sold-out flag, disclosed in data.
- COSPA Atre: Actual COSPA tote and rubber keyring; local stock unconfirmed.
- Donki: Two actual Don Quijote private-label travel accessories, with product pages warning that store inventory differs.
- Eishindo: [Official merchant association](https://www.katsushika-kushouren.jp/store/s35025/) locates this bookstore in Kameari. Kochikame and its Tokyo/Shitamachi companion are publisher-cover examples connected to the neighborhood. Their handling and stock at this shop are unverified and labelled accordingly.
- Kastori: Both books are in the bookstore’s own catalog. The illustrated material is architecture/book covers, not graphic content.
- Kakimori: Both items are current official catalog products. The indigo item is a notebook **cover**, not a promise that a complete notebook/refill is included.
- Nobori: Both horse items are in the shop’s own catalog, with its explicit warning that store and web stock differ. Horse motif selected because the travelers requested horse-related discoveries.
