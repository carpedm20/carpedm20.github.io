## Why these picks

The selection combines Japanese chain signatures with items repeatedly introduced to Korean visitors, plus practical breakfast/drink/beer-side options. This does NOT mean every product ranks among both countries' top sellers.

- FamilyMart's official July 2026 release calls Famichiki its sales No.1 signature and notes support from domestic and inbound customers. This supports the signature choice, without extrapolating sales ranks to other products: https://www.family.co.jp/company/news_releases/2026/20260707_02.html
- FamilyMart's March 2024 release calls souffle pudding a popular dessert with cumulative sales over 80 million. Historical popularity evidence, not a current-year sales claim: https://www.family.co.jp/content/dam/family/company/news_releases/2023/20240319_newsrelease_01.pdf
- Seven's December 2025 Gold-line ranking lists Sumire miso cup ramen fifth; this is the Gold product ranking in that campaign, not all convenience products: https://www.sej.co.jp/var/rev0/0010/9103/PDF.pdf
- Korean travel service 여기어때 recommends egg sandwiches and melon bread among convenience-store snacks (2026-08-20): https://www.yeogi.com/content/ob-jp-all-hotel-bx2-japan-convenience-store-food-best9
